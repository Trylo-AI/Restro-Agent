Who is responsible

When you order from a café on WhatsApp through Restro, the café decides what your data is used for (under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, it is the data fiduciary). Restro ([TO FILL: legal entity name and address]) runs the service for the café and processes your data on its behalf. We never use one café's guest data for another café.

We don't collect payment details: you pay at the café.

The AI assistant

An AI model (Google Gemini on Google Cloud Vertex AI) reads your messages to understand what you'd like. It sees your first name, how many times you've visited, your recent items and the preferences you've stated, never your phone number, your allergies or other guests' data. Prices, totals, availability and your order are always worked out by our software from the café's menu, not by the AI.

Your messages may be processed outside India. We use Google's global endpoint, which doesn't guarantee that processing stays in India. Our database is in Mumbai.

Who else sees your data

  • The café's staff, to serve you. They see your number only partly (for example +91 98••• ••421).
  • Meta (WhatsApp), which carries the messages under WhatsApp's own terms.
  • Our service providers: Google Cloud (hosting and the AI model) and Supabase (database, in Mumbai).

We don't sell your data or use it for advertising outside the café's own offers.

Offers

The café asks once, with Yes and No buttons, whether you'd like offers. Only a Yes lets the café send them. Reply STOP at any time to stop offers immediately; ordering keeps working. If you said No, we won't ask again for at least 30 days.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or erase your data, withdraw consent to offers (reply STOP) and raise a grievance. To erase your data, follow these steps. After erasure the café keeps only anonymous order records it needs for its accounts.

Security

Data travels encrypted. Staff sign in to the console, see only their own café, and every change they make is logged. Access keys are kept in Google Cloud Secret Manager, never in the database or in logs.
