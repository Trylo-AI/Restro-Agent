Delete your data

You can ask a café that uses Restro to erase what it holds about you. Erasure can't be undone.

Only want to stop offers?

Reply STOP in the café's WhatsApp chat. Offers stop at once and you can still order.

How to ask

  1. In the chat: message the café's WhatsApp number, for example "Please delete my data". A person at the café picks it up.
  2. At the café: ask the manager or owner. They can erase your data from the Restro Console.
  3. By email: write to [TO FILL: privacy email] from any address. Tell us the café's name and the WhatsApp number you used, so we can find you; we'll confirm with you on that number before erasing.

We complete a request within [TO FILL: response time]. When the owner erases it in the console, it happens straight away.

What is erased

  • Your name, and your WhatsApp number (replaced by a random code)
  • Your chat messages and the café's replies
  • Your saved preferences and any staff notes about you
  • The text of your feedback

What stays, without your name or number

  • Orders and their items, which the café needs for its accounts
  • Star ratings, offer redemptions and the record of your offer choices, with nothing that identifies you

Backups age out within about 7 days [TO FILL: confirm the backup window]. If a backup is ever restored, completed erasures are applied again before it goes back into use.

After erasure, if you message the café again from the same number, you start as a new guest: no history, and no offers unless you say yes again.
