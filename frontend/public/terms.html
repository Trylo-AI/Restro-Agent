The service

Restro ([TO FILL: legal entity name]) lets you order from a participating café in a WhatsApp chat. The café prepares and serves your food; Restro runs the chat and passes your order to the café's staff.

Your order

  • An order is placed when you tap Confirm. The café accepts it, or tells you if it can't.
  • Prices and totals are the café's menu prices, shown by our software. The AI assistant never sets a price.
  • Dishes can sell out. If something is no longer available, we tell you before or when you confirm.
  • You can change or cancel an order until the café accepts it. After that, ask staff.
  • You pay at the café. Restro doesn't take payments or give refunds; speak to the café.

Allergies and dietary needs

Allergen information comes from the café. If the café hasn't confirmed an allergen for a dish, the assistant won't guess and will pass you to staff. Always tell staff about a serious allergy.

The AI assistant

The assistant can misunderstand. Check the order summary before you tap Confirm. You can ask for a person at any time, and a staff member will reply.

Offers

Offers are sent only if you said yes. An offer code works once, on one bill, at the café that sent it, until the date in the message, on the café's terms. Reply STOP to stop offers.
