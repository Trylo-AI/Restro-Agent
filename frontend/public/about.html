Order at your table on WhatsApp

Restro is ordering and customer care for cafés. Guests scan the QR on their table, order in a WhatsApp chat, get updates as the order is made, and can reach a person at any time.

For guests

  1. Scan the QR on your table. WhatsApp opens a chat with the café.
  2. Tell the assistant what you'd like, in English, Hindi or Hinglish. It shows the menu prices from the café's own menu.
  3. Tap Confirm. You'll get a message when your order is accepted and when it's ready.
  4. Need help? Tap Call waiter or ask for a person. A staff member answers.

The café only sends you offers if you say yes. Reply STOP any time to stop them; ordering keeps working.

For cafés

Staff see every order, request and chat in the Restro Console. The owner sees who came back and what they spent, and can send offers to guests who opted in.
